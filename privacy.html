Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Half Baked Ideas Lab respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or submit an Ideas Recipe, review, or contact message.

What Information We Collect

Depending on what you choose to submit, we may collect your name, email address, the information you provide in an Ideas Recipe, customer reviews, and messages you send to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use information you provide to respond to your requests, process Ideas Recipe submissions, communicate with you about your submission, receive and manage customer reviews, and provide the services offered by Half Baked Ideas Lab.

Email

Information submitted through our website may be transmitted using third-party email services used to deliver messages to Half Baked Ideas Lab. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments and Donations

If you choose to support Half Baked Ideas Lab through Cash App or PayPal, payment information is handled by those payment providers. We do not collect or store your payment card or bank account information through this website.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers necessary to operate our website and deliver communications, such as email services.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

If you have questions about information you have submitted or would like to request that we delete information we have received from you, contact us.
